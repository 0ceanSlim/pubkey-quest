📰 Development Updates

🛒

Monsters, Loot & a Town Full of Shops

October 2026
  • • 36 new monsters for levels 1–5: every road now has something new to meet, from hyenas and giant crabs to kuo-toa, worgs, specters and hippogriffs
  • • Enemy spellcasters — cultists, acolytes, apprentice wizards, cult fanatics and druids — and the wands, orbs and spellbooks they carry
  • • Every monster has a sprite, and every item has art
  • • Loot rebuilt: purses follow the 5e treasure tables, creatures drop their parts (spider silk, bone dust, spirit dust), and the best gear — plate, greatswords, rare foci — is found, never bought
  • • 11 new shopkeepers: the Grand Forge, the Mage Tower, a dwarven crossbow forge, an elven bowyer, an herbalist, the Dawnflame almonry, the Golden Bazaar, an orc war-gear trader, and the first shops in Dusthaven, Frosthold and Saltwind
  • • General stores carry one dependable list of travel basics, and potions come back slowly instead of all at once
  • • Survival matters on the road: it sets how fast you travel and gives you a roll to spot an ambush before it springs. A navigator's kit or a spyglass helps
  • • Useful odds and ends: healer's kits bind wounds, perfume sweetens prices, a tackle box keeps the fish smell contained
  • • A community wiki at /wiki, built straight from the game's data
🗝️

Locks, Secrets & Smarter Fights

October 2026
  • • Keys and locks: locked rooms and chests, a keyring for your keys, lockpicks that break, and Tumblers — a lockpicking minigame
  • • The Rising Shadow is playable: meet the Oracle in the new Temple of the Dawnflame and follow the main quest's first chapter
  • • The Rats of Goldenhaven: down into the sewers to face the Rat King
  • • Hidden places in every city, found by spending time in a district
  • • Combat terrain: rough ground, water, cover and high ground, with line of sight and monsters that use it
  • • Stealth openings: catch monsters unaware and sneak past, strike first, or walk up — or get ambushed yourself
  • • Fight several enemies at once, in true initiative order
  • • Fairer fights at low levels, gentler downing, and death saves you can watch
  • • Use what's at hand: only items in your quick slots are free in combat — digging in your pack costs a bonus action
  • • Lose a fight and you can reload your last save instead of taking the loss
🤝

Friends, Parties & Saves on Nostr

October 2026
  • • See who else is playing: players at your spot, travellers on your road, and a Friends panel built from your Nostr follows
  • • Chat — say, area and party — and private whispers sent as encrypted Nostr DMs, sealed in your browser
  • • Parties of up to five: travel together, sleep together, and fight together in shared turn-based battles with flanking, healing and help actions
  • • Your saves can live on Nostr relays: signed by you, loadable anywhere, checked by the official server
  • • Character portraits — describe one, link one, or upload one — and a unique crest for every npub
  • • Proof-of-key login: you prove you hold your key, and all signing stays in your browser
  • • Your Nostr profile and relays, viewable and editable in game
  • • Quest journal reworked: sortable, filterable, with a badges tab
📜

Feats, Scrolls, Gathering & a Web App

July 2026
  • • Feats — take a feat instead of ability points at level-up (Tough, War Caster, Elemental Adept, and the stat feats)
  • • Spell scrolls — read a scroll to cast its spell in or out of combat (still costs mana, no preparation needed)
  • • Gathering & lockpicking — mining and fishing nodes and locked chests, worked with the right tool and a skill check, with tiered loot
  • • New MILL sign-in — one modal for every Nostr login (extension, Amber, remote bunker, private key, or a brand-new identity); all signing stays in your browser
  • • Installable as an app — add Pubkey Quest to your home screen, and links now unfurl with a preview card
  • • Mobile polish and bug fixes ahead of open testing

The Adventure Comes Alive

June 2026
  • • Quests, dungeons & encounters are playable — pick up quests from NPCs and boards, explore points of interest as branching node-walks, and hit random and hand-authored encounters while travelling
  • • Rotating daily quests handed out by innkeepers
  • • Spellcasting is real — cast spells in and out of combat with mana and material components, and drink potions or throw items mid-fight
  • • Combat depth — class abilities (Rage, Second Wind, Ki…), conditions (poisoned, prone, frightened…), and saving throws
  • • Character progression — level up from XP, spend ability points, and grow your HP and mana
  • • Building interiors — enter inns and taverns, rent a room, and sleep the night

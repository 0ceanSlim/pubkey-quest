📚 About Pubkey Quest

A D&D 5e-inspired RPG in your browser. Sign in with Nostr and your key becomes your hero: race, class, background, ability scores and starting gear all come from your npub. The same key always makes the same character.

What you can do

🗺️ Explore towns, roads and wilds, with day and night, weather and places to find.
⚔️ Fight on a grid with weapons, spells, class abilities and conditions.
📜 Quest through story and side quests, waystones, and dailies from the vault keepers.
🍖 Survive hunger, fatigue, the cold and the heat. Pack food, water and a fire.
🎣 Gather by fishing and mining. Cut gems at the jeweller and have them enchanted.
👥 Play together with friends, chat and parties that travel and fight as one.
🏆 Compete for badges and highscores, kept by the Dungeon Master.
💾 Save when you choose to. No autosave: your progress is what you keep.

Yours, on Nostr

  • Your saves are events you sign, kept on your own relays. The server checks them, never holds your key.
  • Badges are NIP-58 awards from the Dungeon Master, and highscores go to Gamestr.
  • The game is in beta: no wipes. What you earn now stays.

What's next

The main story as one chain from level 1 to 20, quests and fights built for parties, and more art, music and polish. Every change lands in Updates.
